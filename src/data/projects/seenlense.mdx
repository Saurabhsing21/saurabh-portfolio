# Seenlense: AI Visibility Tracker

## Overview

Seenlense (built as AnswerIntel) answers one question for founders and small teams: when buyers ask AI about my category, does it recommend me, and who does it pick instead? It runs realistic buyer questions across AI engines on a schedule and turns the answers into visibility metrics and a fix list.

## Features

- **Distributed scan engine**: FastAPI, Celery and OpenRouter run buyer prompts across 5 LLM engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Grok) with retries and cost tracking.
- **Hybrid analysis pipeline**: An LLM classifier paired with rule-based hallucination guards extracts mentions, ranking, sentiment and citations for share of voice.
- **GEO diagnosis engine**: Audits AI-crawler access via an SSRF-safe fetcher, explains why a brand is missing from AI answers, drafts fixes and re-tests them.
- **Product**: Scheduled re-scans, workspaces, reports and Paddle subscription billing.
- **Architecture**: FastAPI modular monolith with background scan workers, PostgreSQL and JWT auth behind a Next.js same-origin proxy.
